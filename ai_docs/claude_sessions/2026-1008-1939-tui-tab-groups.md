# TUI: tab groups

## Ask

1. From the cats-todo backlog: N-116. Port dbc web's tab groups
   (`web/static/js/tabgroups.js`) to the TUI's query-tab strip. That means
   ad-hoc and connection groups that fold to a chip, plus both later web
   additions:
   - the `+` shows which group a new tab joins, and right-clicking `+` (or a
     group's "New tab in <group>") opens a tab in any group, on that group's
     connection;
   - a connection group with no tab keeps a hollow chip at the strip's end
     (click opens a tab on its connection, right-click opens its menu).
2. `/sess-wrap`.

## Design

The model is the web's, carried over decision by decision. The web module's
header has the full reasoning; ced's `internal/app/tabgroups.go`, a terminal
original of the same feature, served as the TUI reference. These are the
TUI-specific choices:

- **Membership by tab key.** Membership is keyed by `queryTab.key`, the
  identity `tabMsg` already uses. Keys are never reused, so a closed tab's
  stale key matches nothing. Keys are minted fresh each run, so on disk
  (`userdata.LayoutTabGroup`) members and exclusions are stored as indices
  into the saved `Tabs`, which is the order `restoreTabs` rebuilds.
- **Contiguous by reordering.** `arrangeTabs` reorders `m.tabs`, following
  the tab on screen by pointer, and it runs once at the end of `Update`.
  Any message can change a tab's group (a connect landing on a grouped
  connection, a menu row, a close), so one call site beats a dozen. It
  returns at once when there are no groups.
- **Where a tab is going.** `tabConn` reads a connect in flight
  (`ws.Connecting()`) before `tabTarget`. Without that, a tab just opened
  into a connection group reads as being on the workspace's default until
  its connect lands, and gets moved out and back. `newTabIn` likewise adds
  the tab to its group only after `connectCmd` has started the connect.
- **Drawing.** The strip is one border row:
  - A group's chip is filled in its colour, with the editor's background
    as the text colour.
  - Member titles are drawn in the group's colour (the active tab keeps its
    own style) and underlined. The `─` cells between a chip and its members
    are also drawn in the group's colour, so the run reads as one.
  - Idle connection groups are `[name]`, drawn as text only, after the tabs.
  - The `+` is drawn in the colour of the group it lands in, and underlined.
- **Tooltip stand-in.** A terminal has no tooltips, so hovering a group
  chip, an idle chip or `+` writes a muted italic hint after the strip, cut
  to the room left. The hover is kept as a position (`hoverState.strip`)
  rather than a chip index, because the strip is laid out afresh every
  frame.
- **Keyboard door.** ⌥G opens the groups menu: the active tab's
  add/remove rows, then each group's own menu. A right-click is not always
  delivered (macOS Terminal + tmux).
- **Strip with one tab.** The strip now also shows with a single tab when
  any group exists; otherwise an idle group would have nowhere to be seen.
- **Names.** Up to 8 letters or digits, the web's limit rather than ced's 4.
  The TUI strip holds at most 9 tabs.
- **Colours.** The six hues are the web's `--g0…--g5` (app.css). The dark or
  light set is picked by the palette background's brightness, at paint time,
  so a cats theme change restyles the chips too.
- **Edge cases.**
  - Closing a group's tabs skips any session that may hold a transaction,
    and brings back the tab that was on screen.
  - A renamed connection takes its group along (`connRenamed` →
    `renameGroupConns`).
  - A group whose connection was removed refuses "New tab in" with a
    pointer to Ungroup.

## Changes

- `tui/tabgroups.go` (new): the model (`groupOf` / `groupFor`, `arrangeTabs`,
  `landing`, `connFor`, `idleGroups`, the fold), the mutations, the menus
  (group menu, tab rows, Add to group…, the `+` menu, ⌥G), the name prompt,
  and save/restore.
- `tui/tabs.go`:
  - `newTab` → `newTabIn(g)`; `closeTab` forgets the closed tab.
  - `drawTabs` is rewritten (chips, idle chips, the coloured `+`, the
    joining border), and `drawStripHint` is new.
  - `tabChip` gains a kind and a group; `tabClick` / `openTabMenu` take a
    chip.
- `tui/app.go`: `groups` and `savedLayoutGroups` fields; `arrangeTabs` in
  `Update`; ⌥G; the startup keys line.
- `tui/mouse.go`: strip hover. `tui/layout.go`: save and restore groups.
  `tui/connform.go`: rename.
- `userdata/layout.go`: `Layout.Groups`, `LayoutTabGroup`.
- `tui/help.go` and the README's new "Tab groups" section.
- `tui/tabgroups_test.go`: 9 tests covering the connection rule (including
  a tab moving onto the connection and remove/re-add), the ad-hoc group
  (joining, gathering, dropping with its last tab), the fold, the idle chip,
  the `+` landing/hover/right-click, deepest-wins, save/restore and
  connection rename, names and the prompt, and closing a group's tabs.
