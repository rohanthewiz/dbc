# N-210: edge auto-pan for the pipeline canvas's lane drag

Session: `28f89769-fa40-40fc-9db1-d0b3aa9ed25f`

## Ask

N-210 was pasted from the backlog. The lane drag (N-179) did not scroll
the canvas near its top or bottom edge, so a lane could only go as far as
the visible part of the stage. The ask was to add edge auto-pan through the
stage's `tx/ty`, the same way a pan drag sets them. `/sw` came at the end.

## What was done

All the product changes are in `web/static/js/pipelines.js`. `stage.js` is
unchanged.

- **Edge auto-pan** (`laneFollow`, `edgeSpeed`, `autoPan`, after
  `laneDragMove`):
  - The zones are 40px deep (`EDGE`) at the canvas's top and bottom. The
    speed is linear in how deep the pointer is in a zone and reaches
    `PAN_MAX` (900 screen px/s) at the edge and past it. Past the edge
    still works because the canvas captures the pointer.
  - The pan runs on `requestAnimationFrame` and is time-based, with dt
    capped at 50ms. It keeps panning while the pointer is still, and it
    starts only once the drag has moved past the 3px threshold.
  - Bounds on `ty`: `hi = max(ty, VIEW_PAD)` and
    `lo = min(ty, H − geo.h·k − VIEW_PAD)`, where `VIEW_PAD` is 12, fit's
    margin. The stage stops with the pipeline's end 12px inside the
    canvas. A view already past a bound only stops moving that way; it is
    never snapped back. At a bound the loop idles until the pointer leaves
    the zone or the drag ends.
  - The loop ends by itself when `drag !== d`. The drag's `pointerup` and
    `pointercancel` also cancel a pending frame (`d.raf`).
- **The lane follows in stage coordinates.** The press stores `sy` (stage
  y, from `stage.toStage`) and `py` (the latest client y), and
  `laneFollow` passes `toStage(py).y − sy` to `laneDragMove`. The old
  `(clientY − cy)/k` goes stale as soon as the view moves under a still
  pointer. A canvas `wheel` listener calls `laneFollow` during a lane
  drag, so a wheel pan or zoom mid-drag also keeps the lane under the
  pointer. The stage's own wheel listener was registered first (in
  `mount`), so the view has already moved when this listener runs.
  `drag.cy` is still set but no longer read.
- **A race found while testing:** `check()` redraws the canvas when its
  result lands, 500ms after an edit (`checkTimers`). A redraw during a
  lane drag replaced the lifted lane and its `.plmark`. Holding a lane at
  an edge makes this likely, so `check` now skips `renderCanvas` while a
  lane is held. The drag's end always redraws, through `moveFragment` or
  `renderCanvas`. Node and wire drags were left as they were.

## Test

The e2e step `pipelineLaneDrag` (`web/e2e/pipelines_test.go`) gained an
auto-pan block on `manyLanesPipeline(12)`, a new helper that builds
`l00`…`l11`. It first checks that the last lane overflows the canvas, so
the step can't pass vacuously.

1. `l00` is held 4px above the canvas's bottom. The test waits for the
   stage's bottom to settle 12px above the canvas's, holds for 200ms to
   check the pan doesn't overshoot, and takes a screenshot
   (`pipeline-lane-autopan`). After the drop, `l00` is last.
2. `l00` is held 4px below the canvas's top. The test waits for the
   stage's top to sit 12px below the canvas's. After the drop, `l00` is
   first.

The first debug run showed the pan working at about 810 px/s and stopping
at the bound within 0.1px. It also showed the lifted lane lost about
0.6s into the hold, which turned out to be the check race above.
